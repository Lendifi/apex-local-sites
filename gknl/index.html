How a GKNL clinic visit runs

Steps taken from GKNL's own clinic staff job posts (2023).VERIFY current flow

1

Check in

Staff greet you, check you in and get your file ready.

2

Paperwork, with help

Someone walks you through the forms so nothing is missed.

3

Height, weight, blood pressure

Quick vitals before you see the doctor.

4

Your medical history

Staff take down your history for the exam.

5

Exam with the assigned doctor

The doctor does the exam the state asked for. They do not decide your claim or prescribe treatment.

6

The report goes to the state

Paperwork is organized and sent on. The state agency reviews it with the rest of your file and decides.
