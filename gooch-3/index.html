What goeson the lift

Everything here comes from Gooch's own Google listing and public reviews. Tap "Add to estimate" on any plate and it lands in the form at the bottom.

Diesel and engine

On the list
Diesel trucks and engines. Complete engine replacements and rebuilds.
Good time to call
It will not start, smokes, or has lost its power.

Transmission and gears

On the list
Transmission replacements and rebuilds. Differential gear swaps and locker installs.
Good time to call
Shifts feel off, or you changed tire size and want to re-gear.

Shocks and suspension

On the list
Shock rebuilds and recharge, to get the suspension back to full strength.
Good time to call
The ride has gone floaty, harsh or leaky.

Lifts, levels and builds

On the list
Lift and level kits. Aftermarket part installs. Off-road builds.
Good time to call
You want more tire room or a new stance, or a lift you already have needs a second look.

Fabrication and welding

On the list
Custom fabrication and welding. Also listed: powder coating, CNG and small engine work.
Good time to call
The part you need is not sold off the shelf.

Maintenance, brakes and checks

On the list
Oil changes, A/C recharge, tire rotation, brake work, diagnostics and electrical, pre-purchase inspections.
Good time to call
A light comes on, the brakes feel soft, or you are about to buy a used truck.

Also on their listing: cars, motorcycles and heavy equipment. "Good time to call" lines are plain-language prompts written for this concept, not Gooch's wording.
