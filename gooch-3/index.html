What goeson the lift

1 of 6

Diesel and engine

On the list
Diesel trucks and engines. Complete engine replacements and rebuilds.
Good time to call
It will not start, it smokes, or it has lost its power.

Transmission and gears

On the list
Transmission replacements and rebuilds. Differential gear swaps and locker installs.
Good time to call
Shifts feel off, or you changed tire size and want to re-gear.

Shocks and suspension

On the list
Shock rebuilds and recharge, to get the suspension back to full strength.
Good time to call
The ride has gone floaty, harsh or leaky.

Lifts, levels and builds

On the list
Lift and level kits, aftermarket parts and full off-road builds.
Good time to call
You want more clearance, a level stance, or a build planned around how you drive.

Fabrication and welding

On the list
Custom fabrication and welding. Powder coating, CNG and small engine work are listed too.
Good time to call
You need a part that does not exist yet, or one that broke.

Maintenance, brakes and checks

On the list
Oil changes, A/C recharge, tire rotation, brake work, diagnostics and electrical, pre-purchase inspections.
Good time to call
A light is on, a service is due, or you are about to buy a used vehicle.
