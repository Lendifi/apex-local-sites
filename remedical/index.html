Questions we hear a lot

Answers pulled from ReMedical's own pages and posts.

What symptoms can low testosterone cause?

ReMedical lists brain fog, fatigue, low libido, loss of motivation, loss of muscle mass, weight gain, irritability, poor focus and memory, and more. Only a provider can tell you if TRT fits you.

Can testosterone be too high?

Yes. As ReMedical puts it, "both too little or too high testosterone can cause problems," which is why every plan is customized to reach the correct level.

Do you treat women's hormones?

Yes. Re-Place™ Hormone Therapy treats menopausal and perimenopausal symptoms, balancing estrogen, progestin, and testosterone under a licensed provider trained in women's health.

Is ReMedical still offering HRT?

Yes. In their words: "We love HRT. We aren't ever giving it up."

How does medical weight loss work here?

A thorough screening, including blood tests, to see if you are a good candidate for semaglutide or tirzepatide. If you are, the team prescribes and monitors your medication. Dr. J lost over 100 lbs on the same protocol.

Brand name or compounded GLP-1?

ReMedical says compounded GLP-1s can allow more flexible dosing based on how your body responds, and that where your medication comes from matters. Your provider will walk you through the options.

Is the consultation free?

Yes, consultations are free and payment plans are available. Call 801-807-8811 or text 801-823-2978.
