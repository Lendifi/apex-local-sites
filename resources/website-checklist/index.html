The 15-point local website checklist.

Grab your phone and give yourself about 15 minutes. Every box you can't check is a place customers might be slipping away. Print this page or save it as a PDF from your browser.

Have Sam run it for me
  • 1. Phone number visible at the top on a phone
    Open your site on your phone. You should see your number without scrolling.
  • 2. Tap-to-call works
    Tap the number. It should start a call, not do nothing.
  • 3. Padlock shows, no "Not secure" warning
    Look at the address bar on your phone and computer.
  • 4. Site loads from your Google profile link
    Search your business on Google and tap Website.
  • 5. Mobile speed checked
    Run your address through pagespeed.web.dev and look at the mobile score.
  • 6. Quote form reaches you
    Send yourself a test. Check the inbox and spam folder.
  • 7. Services listed in plain words
    Use the words customers search, like "sprinkler repair."
  • 8. Cities you serve written out
    Name each city, such as Lehi, Saratoga Springs, and American Fork.
  • 9. Real photos of your work
    Swap stock photos for your own jobs, crew, and shop.
  • 10. Reviews shown or linked
    Let visitors see what customers say about you.
  • 11. No leftover template text
    No "Lorem ipsum," fake addresses, or sample phone numbers.
  • 12. Hours, phone, and copyright year current
    Fix anything stale in the header and footer.
  • 13. Domain in your name, renewals on a current card
    Know where it's registered and when it renews.
  • 14. You have the logins
    Website and domain logins saved somewhere safe.
  • 15. Google Business Profile matches your site
    Same name, phone, services, and cities on both.

Scored less than 12? Start with anything that stops people from reaching you: the site not loading, security warnings, a broken form, or a wrong number. Then work down the list. For the why behind each item, read the 10-minute website check, and for Google, see our Google Business Profile tips.
