What goes in the folder

Four parts of every clinic day, taken from how GKNL describes its own clinics.VERIFY

Part 1

The exam

Basic physicals done by licensed physicians, with NPs and PAs where the state allows.VERIFY The exam the state agency asked for.

Part 2

Vitals and history

Clinic staff take height, weight and blood pressure and record the claimant's medical history before the exam.

Part 3

The report

Paperwork is organized after each exam and sent back to headquarters, then on to the state agency. The goal, in Jonas's words: the highest quality of medical reports.

Part 4

Respect for the claimant

Help with forms, a steady schedule, and every claimant treated with respect and dignity.
